    Asked at the counter.

    We showed this to owners before we built it. These were their questions.

    “Where do your prices come from?”

    From each cafe's own menu. We read it on their website, or we take a photo of it in the shop. Next to every price you can see the date we checked it and a link to that menu.

    “Why is it free? What is the catch?”

    There is no catch. Counter is free while we start out: find your shop, pick your competitors and use every page, from your prices to their new items and offers. The Weekly Bake is free too.

    “So what is the S$20 a month for?”

    For what we build next: more items to compare, a message the morning a competitor changes a price, and your prices and theirs over time. You pay nothing today. Join the waitlist and we email you when it starts.

    “What if you have my price wrong?”

    Tell us and we fix it. Reply to The Weekly Bake, or send us a photo of your menu.

    Send your menu

    “My shop is not on your list.”

    Add it yourself in about a minute: type your shop’s name and postal code, then upload a photo or PDF of your menu.

    Many owners told us the same. They use it for other decisions: what to charge for a new item, what cafes near them sell that they don't, and who is running a discount.
