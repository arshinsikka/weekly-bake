    What owners asked us

    We showed this to cafe owners at their counters. These were their questions.

    “Where do your prices come from?”

    From each cafe's own menu. We read it on their website, or we take a photo of it in the shop. Next to every price you can see the date we checked it and a link to that menu.

    “Why is it free? What is the catch?”

    There is no catch. You can look up your shop, see how your flat white and croissant prices compare with other cafes, use the map, and get our email every week. All of that is free.

    “So what is the $20 a month for?”

    “What if you have my price wrong?”

    Tell us and we change it. Reply to our email, or send us a photo of your menu.

      Where the numbers come from

      We only use each cafe's own menu, online or in the shop. If a menu looks old, we leave it out. We also read two government lists. We do not copy from delivery apps and we do not buy social media data. Everything here is public, or the shop sent it to us.

      What is coming

      From November we will show which new items got a discount soon after they started, and which ones left the menu. For this we must watch the same shops for a few months. We are doing that now.
